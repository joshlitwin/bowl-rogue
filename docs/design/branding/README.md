# A real name for Advancing Ten-Pin (22 November 2026)

"bowl-rogue" is the working title and "Advancing" the mode name. This folder is a review board of name directions (three rounds of five), each with a wordmark, a 16x16 pixel icon, colours, a tagline and a 1200x630 share card. Nothing here has been applied to the game or the front page yet.

- Board: [`index.html`](index.html) (one self-contained page; `?card=<id>` shows one share card at 1200x630)
- Screenshots: [`board.png`](board.png) (desktop), [`board-phone.png`](board-phone.png) (390 wide)
- Per direction: `<id>/icon.svg` (favicon / app icon source, pure pixels, no fonts), `<id>/icon-180.png` (apple-touch-icon size), `<id>/card.png` (og:image)

## Round 3: Toronto alleys (22 November, after Josh passed on round 2)
Josh's note: "Go less cutesy and smart. Something like the name of a street or bowling alley related to the history of Toronto bowling." The history research, with sources and the unconfirmed points marked, is in [`toronto-history.md`](toronto-history.md). Each name is styled as a real sign of its period. On the board, round 3 is at the top and rounds 1 and 2 are folded below it.

| Name | History (sources in toronto-history.md) | Look | Tagline | Checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Temperance Lanes** ([card](temperance-lanes/card.png)) | By one account, Tommy Ryan's first regulation ten-pin alley was above a store at Yonge and Temperance; five-pin came out of his Toronto Bowling Club in 1909 (same address not confirmed) | The game's own Rec Room sign: Pixelify Sans, wall brown, orange and cream stripes | Ten pins. Know when to stop. | No live alley with this name found (only Forest View Lanes in Temperance, Michigan). No game with this name; several itch.io games are called just "Temperance" |
| **Toronto Bowling Club** ([card](toronto-bowling-club/card.png)) | Ryan's club: first regulation ten-pin lanes 1905, five-pin around 1909, first five-pin league 1910 | Gold leaf on black glass, IM Fell English SC | Ten-pin alleys. Yonge Street, 1905. | Some risk. No live alley by this name, but Toronto lawn-bowling clubs have close names, and the App Store is full of "Bowling Club" apps |
| **Home Bowling Club** ([card](home-bowling-club/card.png)) | Second-floor alley at Queen East and Parliament, about 1925–1978; entrance uncovered in 2017 | Painted ghost sign on brick, Alfa Slab One | Second floor. Ten pins. Open late. | Some risk. Nothing live found; same "Bowling Club" app crowding. Steam and itch.io not searched separately |
| **O'Connor Bowl** ([card](oconnor-bowl/card.png)) | East York alley, 65 years, demolished for condos in 2015 (address and opening year not confirmed) | Postwar neon: pink Yellowtail script, cyan block letters, After Close colours | Open late. Ten pins waiting. | Low risk. No live alley found; app results are only college-football bowls. A family name. Steam and itch.io not searched separately |
| **Queen City Lanes** ([card](queen-city-lanes/card.png)) | Toronto's old nickname; a Queen City Bowling Club photo on Church Street about 1906 (lawn or alleys not confirmed) | Bulb-lit deco marquee, Limelight | Ten pins. One long night. | Low risk. No alley or game with this name; Google Play has a developer called "QUEEN CITY". "Queen City" also names other cities |

Dropped: Shamrock Bowl (live, reopened 2019), any Bowlerama (live chain), Bayview Bowl (Bay View Bowl in Milwaukee is a live alley), Danforth Bowl and Kennedy Bowl (open in 2017, treated as live), King Edward Lanes (live hotel brand).

**Round 3 recommendation: Temperance Lanes.** It names five-pin's home corner in a way that reads like a real sign. No business uses the name now. It needs no new look: the game's Rec Room sign already is that sign, so applying it changes words only. The word suits the game's choice between knocking pins now and holding back, without being a pun. Runner-up: O'Connor Bowl (a real postwar alley whose neon sign matches the After Close hall).

Fonts added in round 3 (Google Fonts): IM Fell English SC, Alfa Slab One, Yellowtail, Limelight.

## Round 2: less literal (22 November, after Josh passed on round 1)
Josh's note on round 1: "None of these. Go less literal." Round 2 drops names that describe the mechanic (no ripen, creep, frame, burst, pin, rack, bowl) and goes for the feel: a late-night hall, what you leave coming back, patience against greed. The tagline carries the bowling. On the board this section is at the top; round 1 is folded underneath it.

About 40 names were brainstormed; 21 were searched; 16 dropped for existing games. Shortlist:

| Name | What it's about | Tagline | Name check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comeuppance** ([card](comeuppance/card.png)) | A phrase with a sting: greed gets paid back, what you leave comes back for you. Pixel blackletter, oxblood and brass | The pins you leave come back. | Low risk. No game or app with this title on Steam, App Store, Google Play or itch.io; results were articles about revenge in games. Long (11 letters) and often misspelled |
| **Latewood** ([card](latewood/card.png)) | A borrowed word: the dense, dark ring a tree grows late in the season. Pins are maple, bowlers call pins "wood", and it's late at night. Icon: tree rings | Ten pins. Leave them late. | Low risk. Nothing named Latewood found. Near in sound: Rotwood (a roguelike), Fellwood (Steam), Last Wood. A dictionary word, so a weaker trademark than a coined one |
| **Sodium Hour** ([card](sodium-hour/card.png)) | A mood and a time: only the orange sodium lamps still on. One-hue identity | Ten pins under the last lamp. | Low risk. No "Sodium Hour" or "Sodium Lights" game on Steam or itch.io; the single word Sodium is an existing game, and a Steam Workshop item is called Sodium Lights. App Store and Google Play not searched separately |
| **Loiter** ([card](loiter/card.png)) | One short verb: hanging about after hours, as the pins you leave do. Dot-matrix score display | Leave a pin. It won't leave. | Low risk for an exact match (none found), but search results fill with "Looter" games one letter away; an ordinary verb, hard to own |
| **Open Tab** ([card](open-tab/card.png)) | An object with a double meaning: a bar tab you don't settle runs up and comes due at closing. Receipt on felt | Leave pins standing. Settle up later. | Some risk. No game found, but the phrase is everywhere in software (browser tabs), so it's hard to find or own. Google Play not searched separately |

Dropped in round 2 after a search: Mothlight, Highwater, Tenfold, Late Bloom, Ghost Light, Windfall, Overstay, Hush Hour, Nightcap, Neon Debt, Small Hours, Gloaming, Dimmer, Wolf Hour, Starlite, Slow Burn (each has a game already; links on the board). Brainstormed but not searched: Last Orders, Velvet Hour, Owl Shift, Halflight, Afterglow, Last Call, Moth Hour, Undertow, Encore, Linger, Kept, Keepsake, Nightkeep, Let It Ride, Compound, Brass Neck, Threshold, Lamp Oil.

**Round 2 recommendation: Latewood.** One word, three readings that fit without spelling out the rules (wood = pins, late = after hours and leaving pins standing, latewood = the strongest wood, grown by waiting). Nothing turned up under it. The ring icon looks like no other bowling game's and reads at 16 px. Runner-up: Comeuppance (strongest mood, but long and easy to misspell).

Fonts added in round 2 (Google Fonts): Jacquard 24, DotGothic16, Tiny5, Doto, Major Mono Display.

Searches were web searches for each name with Steam, App Store, Google Play and itch.io; no trademark database (USPTO, CIPO) was searched. Run one on the chosen name before spending money on it.

**Round 1 recommendation (superseded): Ripe Rack.** It names the decision you make every ball, reads as bowling, turned up nothing, and uses the game's own fonts (Pixelify Sans, Silkscreen) and Rec Room colours, so applying it changes words and icons, not the look. Runner-up: Pin Creep.

## Fonts
Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen (already in the game), Jersey 10 (Pin Creep), VT323 (Long Frame), all Google Fonts. In SVG and CSS, "Jersey 10" must be quoted (`'Jersey 10'`): unquoted, the "10" makes the family name invalid and the browser falls back. Pinburst's letters are hand-drawn 5x7 pixels in the page's script, no font.

## Rendering
Google Fonts can't be reached from Claude's sandbox, so the PNGs were rendered with the same fonts installed locally (from the @fontsource npm packages) and headless Chrome:
`google-chrome --headless=new --window-size=1200,630 --screenshot=card.png "index.html?card=ripe-rack"`.
`*.png` is in `.gitignore`; the PNGs here were added with `git add -f` because they are deliverables (og:image, apple-touch-icon).

## What applying the chosen name would touch (not done yet)
1. **Root `index.html`**: `<title>`, the sign's `h1` ("bowl-rogue" and its "a bowling roguelike" line), the card's "Advancing" label. Swap the sign's disc for the new icon if wanted.
2. **Share meta tags** in the root `index.html` (and the game page if it is shared directly): `og:title`, `og:description` (the tagline), `og:image` and `twitter:image` with an **absolute** URL to `card.png` (crawlers need PNG/JPG, not SVG), `og:image:width` 1200 / `og:image:height` 630, `og:url`, `twitter:card` = `summary_large_image`. Copy the card to a stable path such as `/brand/card.png`.
3. **Favicon and home-screen icon**: `<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="brand/icon.svg">`, a 32x32 PNG fallback, and `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="brand/icon-180.png">` (iOS needs PNG; the `icon-180.png` files here are 16 pixels scaled by 11.25, slightly soft at the edges; for the final one render at 11x (176) and pad to 180 with the background colour). Put these on the root page and `labs/advancing-ten-pin/`.
4. **Web manifest** (`manifest.webmanifest`): `name`, `short_name`, `theme_color`/`background_color` from the palette, icons 192 and 512 PNG (plus a `maskable` one with padding), `display: standalone`, `start_url` pointing at the game. Lets "Add to Home Screen" use the name and icon.
5. **The game's title text**: `<title>Advancing Ten-Pin</title>` in `labs/advancing-ten-pin/index.html` and any title or sign drawn in the hall. That file is being changed on another branch, so do this after it merges.
6. **README.md** heading and first line, the "Advancing Ten-Pin" row, `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/STATE.md` mentions. Keep "Advancing" as the mode name inside docs if useful.
7. **Keep the save key** `advten-save` as it is. Saves live in `localStorage` per origin (`joshlitwin.github.io`), so a new path keeps them, but renaming the key would drop every saved run.
8. **Repo name and Pages URL**: the game is served at https://joshlitwin.github.io/bowl-rogue/. Renaming the GitHub repo changes that URL to `joshlitwin.github.io/<new-name>/`, and [GitHub does not redirect project-site URLs after a rename](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/renaming-a-repository) (git remotes and web links to the repo are redirected; the Pages URL is not). Any shared link to the old URL would 404. Options: keep the repo name and only rebrand the pages; rename and leave a tiny `bowl-rogue` repo with a redirect page; or use a custom domain, which GitHub recommends because it doesn't change when the repo is renamed. Update the URL in `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` if it changes.
